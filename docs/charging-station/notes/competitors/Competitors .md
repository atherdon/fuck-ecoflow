## Entries

### ALLPOWERS VOLIX P300

Portable power station sold by Allpowers Ukraine. The product page lists 256 Wh capacity, a 300 W AC outlet (peak 600 W) with a pure sine wave, and a LiFePO4 battery (over 4,000 cycles). It also lists a 1-hour AC charge (up to 300 W in), UPS switchover of 20 ms, and a weight of 3.57 kg. Listed price: ₴9,499, with a regular price of ₴15,577.

### Powerone+ stations on Shvydko

Four listings from the same shop (shvydko.com.ua), all branded Powerone+:

- **TVM 3000 W, 840 Wh** — AGM, 12 V 70 Ah / 840 Wh, 3000 W modified sine, 20 kg, listed at 26,990 UAH. Charge time listed as 6 hours.
- **TVM 3000 W, 1680 Wh, pure sine** — AGM, 12 V 140 Ah / 1680 Wh, 3000 W pure sine, 20 kg, listed at 31,990 UAH. Charge time listed as 6–10 hours.
- **TVM 3000 W LiFePO4, 1680 Wh** — LiFePO4, 12 V 140 Ah / 1680 Wh, 3000 W pure sine, 12 kg, listed at 42,000 UAH and out of stock. The page highlights a 2–3 hour (also listed as 2 hour) charge from 0 to 100%.
- **TVM 2000 W, 840 Wh** — AGM, 12 V 70 Ah / 840 Wh, 2000 W modified sine, 20 kg, listed at 26,490 UAH. Charge time listed as 6 hours.

All four list two AC 220 V outlets, one USB 5 V / 2.4 A output, and a 12 V car socket. Dimensions are listed as 241×450×321 mm.

### Oleksii Vyshnevsky / R&D Things (Focus.ua, 21 December 2022)

A news article, not a shop listing. It describes a portable battery built by Lviv engineer Oleksii Vyshnevsky with the R&D Things team: 2.8 kWh in a metal case, with a battery, inverter, and charger, 220 V sockets and USB, and water/shock protection. The article gives a production cost of 16,000 UAH at the time, and a full charge time of about a day. Chemistry and output wattage are not stated. This is historical reporting from 2022, not a current price.

### BOSECO ENERGY

Ukrainian company (Odesa) that builds portable 220 V battery packs, including custom units. The home page describes backup power for apartments, houses, and appliances up to 1.5 kW, plus solar panels. The FAQ uses model names such as EM 300/256, EM 1000/1000, and the BPS series (described as having a correct sine wave). No prices or a single standard chemistry figure were listed on the home page.

### LiFePO4 12 V 200 Ah EVE BOX (MDS Battery)

A 12 V 200 Ah LiFePO4 battery pack, not a complete charging station. The page says it is built on EVE LF105 3.2 V 105 Ah cells, with a JK Smart Bluetooth BMS (200 A discharge, charge up to 100 A) and a 2 A active balancer. Listed price: 28,900 UAH (original price 32,000 UAH). Sold by MDS Battery in Kyiv. No inverter output is stated.

### EnergyUP

The linked page is an online shop for **electric-vehicle chargers** (portable EV cables, wallboxes, and commercial EV stations), not a portable household power station. No single capacity, output, chemistry, or price applies to that landing page.

### LLE charging stations

LLE is a Ukrainian company that both sells and manufactures charging stations, including custom builds. The catalog also lists EcoFlow models. Figures in some catalog cards do not match the title (for example a "МІЦЬ" card whose title and description disagree), so the table does not treat those conflicting numbers as facts.

Title-level figures that were consistent on the catalog for LLE's own models include:

- ЗС МАЛЮК 1 — 600 Wh / 600 W, LiFePO4, 18,000 UAH (listed out of stock)
- ЗС МАЛЮК 2 — 800 Wh / 1,800 W, LiFePO4, 31,000 UAH (listed out of stock)
- ЗС LLE МІЦЬ 3600 W – 7200 W — battery 3600 W and inverter 3600 W as listed on the card, LiFePO4, 85,000 UAH

EcoFlow River 2, River 2 Max, River 2 Pro, and Delta 2 also appear on the same page, at 13,500–47,999 UAH as listed.

### Casper 200 Ah UPS module (Bezpeka Veritas)

Module for electronic-warfare equipment: manufacturer listed as Casper, sold by Bezpeka Veritas. The page lists a LiFePO4 battery, 200 Ah (also 200,000 mAh), 25 V output, 50 A charge current, and a price of 72,576 UAH. No output wattage is stated. The same URL was on the original list twice.

### MIWARS 2.5 kWh UPS for EW (SKYHUB)

Uninterruptible power supply aimed at electronic-warfare loads. The SKYHUB page lists 2500 Wh (105 Ah), LiFePO4, 1000 W maximum load, stabilized 28 V output, XT90 connectors, a 3–4 hour AC charge, about 2,800 cycles, 23 kg, and a listed price of 38,160 UAH.

### AMAGA battery module (Prom.ua)

The original Prom.ua link is kept. When the page was opened on 26 November 2026, it redirected to a general drone-parts category and did not show the AMAGA product, so capacity, output, chemistry, and price could not be verified.

### NMC moisture-proof stations (Prom.ua, VoltCraft Dynamics)

Two related listings, both with manufacturer listed as NMC:

- **3.67 kWh** — Li-NMC battery, 2 kW nominal inverter, pure sine, 24 V system, moisture- and shock-resistant case, 37,000 UAH. The page also mentions an optional extra 1.5 kWh battery and a 0.5 kW/h charger.
- **1.55 kWh** — Li-NMC battery, 1 kW nominal inverter with a square wave ("прямокутний синус"), 24 V system, 16,000 UAH, listed as unavailable. The listing title also says "інвертор 1\2 кВт". Charge is listed at 0.12 kW/h, full charge in 12 hours.

### Kseonics Technology and DDSR V3

[Kseonics Technology](https://www.kseonics-technology.com.ua/) is a Ukrainian manufacturer (Mlyniv / Rivne area). The company site says it makes portable charging stations and drone-detection systems. The homepage mentions a pure sine inverter of 2000 W (4000 W surge) and a weight of 22 kg, but does not name a charging-station model or give a capacity or price.

The second link is a specific product: **DDSR V3**, a drone-detection system (not a charging station), listed at 10,000 UAH. The page lists Li-Ion 20,000 mAh, up to 16 hours of runtime, IP65, and a weight of 1,450 g.
