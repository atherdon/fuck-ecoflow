# Versions timeline

A compact reading of the planned versions. The original notes are on [Versions](./Versions.md).

## Нуль (0)

The first box: the case itself, a BMS, and a lead to charge the batteries from outside. Room or cables for extra USB outputs.

A Dell power brick from OLX is mentioned for charging the pack. No cooling and no extra protection at the start. A few wires can be enough to charge tablets.

The pack can be an FPV battery already on hand, or ordinary cells bought for the purpose.
