# Plastic Corner Protection - Implementation Task

## Objective
Design and implement protective plastic corners for ammo box-based charging cases to absorb impact and improve durability during ground contact and drone delivery operations.

## Background & Context

### Challenge
- "Limited" availability of Mavic 3 cases for scaling production to 100+ units
- Commercial alternatives cost ~$40 per unit (UA welding company)
- Weight optimization critical for Vampire drone delivery operations

### Proposed Solution
Ammo boxes provide an optimal form factor with benefits including:
- Cost-effective procurement (currently inexpensive in Ukraine)
- Enhanced heat management capabilities
- Easy transportation and handling
- Manageable weight considerations

![image.png](image.png)

## Approach

### Design Strategy
Plastic corners will serve a **functional, not aesthetic purpose**:
- Absorb impact on 1-2 corner points during ground contact
- Protect case integrity during delivery operations
- Enable rapid scaling and resource-efficient manufacturing

### Implementation Method
- Corners will be adhered using industrial-grade adhesive
- Designed for quick production and assembly
- Prioritizes practicality and scalability over custom fabrication
