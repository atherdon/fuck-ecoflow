Problem: we didnt have a lot of mavic 3 cases in order to produce 100+ charging cases.

Another option is to buy locally cases from UA welding company. But it will cost around $40 each.

Simple solution is to buy ammo boxes. Come very cheap in Ukraine right now.

![image.png](image.png)

Ammo box is a fine form factor, as it can help with the heat management and its easy to tow. But comes with problems. Metal case adding some weight that is not good for a delivery from a Vampire drone. Still we didnt have a lot of room, so we will buy around 200 of those ammo boxes and build smaller version of charging stations. I want to put at least 4 plastic corners outside the box. But maybe we should put some corners inside of the box to prevent batteries to hit.

Main goal that one or two corners will absorb the hit of the ground. So its not about being a fancy thing. But more about practicallity. My idea was to put them on a glue. Rob is against it.

But in terms of doing it quickly - scaling, resource management, etc - im fine with corners as a solution.

Still im open for a suggestions about it.

# Plastic Corner Protection - Implementation Task

## Objective
Design and implement protective plastic corners for ammo box-based charging cases to absorb impact and improve durability during ground contact and drone delivery operations.

## Background & Context

### Challenge
- Limited availability of Mavic 3 cases for scaling production to 100+ units
- Commercial alternatives cost ~$40 per unit (UA welding company)
- Weight optimization critical for Vampire drone delivery operations

### Proposed Solution
Ammo boxes provide an optimal form factor with benefits including:
- Cost-effective procurement (currently inexpensive in Ukraine)
- Enhanced heat management capabilities
- Easy transportation and handling
- Manageable weight considerations

## Approach

### Design Strategy
Plastic corners will serve a **functional, not aesthetic purpose**:
- Absorb impact on 1-2 corner points during ground contact
- Protect case integrity during delivery operations
- Enable rapid scaling and resource-efficient manufacturing

### Implementation Method
- Corners will be adhered using industrial-grade adhesive
- Designed for quick production and assembly
- Prioritizes practicality and scalability over custom fabrication
