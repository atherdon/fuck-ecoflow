# Box "Physics"

This idea is already about a year old. There is a lot of information, many materials, but very little progress.

### Concept:

- a case from a Mavic 3T drone;
- 3–4 FPV batteries inside;
- control board / charging board;
- laptop power supply;
- GaN Hota USB hub with XT60;
- a cooler;
- a power button.

# How it works now

Right now the Germans buy new EcoFlow units for us.

Then:

1. They are sent to the Netherlands, where we have a logistics hub.
2. Then everything is loaded into the car of our "smuggler" — a military chaplain.
3. He brings it into Ukraine.
4. Then in Chernivtsi there is a logistics hub of the Ukrainian foundation.
5. There, everything is repackaged and sent to different units.

---

# How it could be

Instead of ready-made EcoFlow units:

- a batch of wires is purchased;
- the necessary boards are purchased;
- Mavic cases;
- electronics;
- components.

All of this goes to Ukraine, and here we assemble and distribute the finished solutions among the units.
