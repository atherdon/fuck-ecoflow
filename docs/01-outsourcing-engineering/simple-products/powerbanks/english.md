#### Example of use

Every night the "Vampires" deliver packages to the infantry.

There is a power bank in each package.

A regular power bank costs at least 1400 UAH. But at the front it is effectively single-use:

- the infantry charges their radio from it;
- after that the power bank is often simply thrown away.

Recently the Dutch handed us 47 such used power banks. I gave them to an infantry company.

For them it is:

- a significant cost saving;
- a free solution for the military;
- the ability to cover a basic power need.

Yes, these power banks are not perfect. They break quickly. But the main thing — they work.
