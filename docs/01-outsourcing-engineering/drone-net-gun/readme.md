# Drone — Net Launcher (Cost estimates)

Status: Draft — cost-focused notes for early-stage evaluation.

## Summary
This file contains a simple cost estimate for using disposable nets with a drone-mounted launcher. These are rough figures for budgeting and risk assessment only.

## Assumptions
- Cost per net: $25
- "Flight" means one deployment of a net
- 10 flights is a short trial; 300 flights approximates one month of regular use
- Does not include training, replacement drone units, maintenance, or other operational costs

## Cost breakdown
- 1 net: $25
- 10 flights: $25 × 10 = $250
- 300 flights (approx. one month of work): $25 × 300 = $7,500

## Notes & Risks
- High recurring consumable cost if nets are single-use.
- Additional expenses not included: training, spare parts, replacement drones (losses), launcher system maintenance, and logistics.
- Operational risk: possibility of losing a drone when using a launcher system.

## Conclusion
