# Glossary

Short definitions of terms that appear often in these docs. They follow the way the pages already use the words.

- **Ящик / case** — the rugged charging box this project is building: a kit you can open and start working from, with batteries, cables and room for tablets packed together.
- **EcoFlow (єкофлоу, єкофлошка)** — a commercial portable power station. Positions often depend on one today; the case is meant to reduce that dependence.
- **Фальш-кришка / false lid** — a panel inside the case lid used to mount equipment or close off the working area.
- **BMS** — a battery management system that watches charge, discharge and cell balance.
- **Li-ion / LiFePO4 (lifepo, ліфери)** — battery chemistries discussed for the case. LiFePO4 is often mentioned as easier to repair and less prone to fire than Li-ion.
- **Павербанк** — a USB power bank, often carried separately from the tablet today.
- **FPV** — first-person-view drones, and the batteries and chargers they use.
- **HOTA** — a commercial charging hub used with some drone batteries. One task explores charging Matrice 4 batteries without it.
- **Matrice 4** — a DJI drone family whose batteries need a compatible charging hub.
- **White box** — a do-it-yourself or unbranded charging-station build, discussed next to EcoFlow.
- **Фізік / Physicist** — the name used for this case design (“Ящик фірми Фізік і друзі”) and for the partner workshop around it.
- **Пост / position** — a field position (reconnaissance, bomber, or FPV) that has to be set up, powered, and packed down.
- **Кілл-зона / kill zone** — the area around a position that vehicles cannot safely enter, so gear has to be carried by hand.
- **Mavic** — DJI reconnaissance drones, and the operators who carry their own tablets and controllers.
- **Вампір / Vampire** — heavy bomber-drone positions in the same unit.
- **QC 3** — Quick Charge 3.0 boards used as charging outputs.
- **XT60** — a common DC power connector, including USB-to-XT60 leads.
- **Інвертор / inverter** — a device that turns the case battery’s DC into AC (for example 220 V).
