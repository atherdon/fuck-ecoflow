Side story: when I was 22, I started my own outsourcing company.

We didn't steal clients. We didn't overprice or fool anyone.

The first project was $50. We did it for a month. Just to get a 5-star review.

I was living in my woman friend's apartment and coding. My partner coded in his one-room apartment. His father was an alcoholic and slept on the floor.

It was his idea. I thought, why not? It would be interesting.

I ate cheap tomatoes with sour cream. Smoked and drank energy drinks.

My record in that 0-office was 22 hours of straight coding.

I learned English only because I had no other choice. If I couldn't convince my client, I wouldn't get paid.

Our market was the worldwide market. A very competitive place, I would say.

This is how we collected our first $900 to open our first office.

Our window faced a sidewall. At the bottom was a huge trash bin.

Five years later, we failed.

Two unfinished projects. Salary debt.

My partner and I returned to our small apartments and started coding again.

I finished one project for our Colorado client in three months.

My partner completed his project in six months.

$0 paid.

And at that time, it was 2014. So I was paying rent for three families.

Me + granny.
Mother.
Aunt + her husband.

Our result: ~$236k moved into Ukraine using our intellect.

Not big. But I own it with pride.

And -$40k in failed projects.

Our biggest success was a project that generated 25–28% of that money for us.

The next ones brought maybe 6%, 6%, and 3%.

So it was a relentless grind.
