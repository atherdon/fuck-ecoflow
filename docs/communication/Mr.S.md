A new 7 kWh power station for Ukraine

Eyes on Ukraine has added another high-capacity power station to its growing arsenal.

This time, we've built a 7 kWh system based on a Pecron E2400LFP, expanded with a 5 kWh golf cart battery and a custom-made connection cable.

The test results exceeded our expectations. The system combines a large energy capacity with fast charging and high continuous output, making it well suited for powering equipment in locations where there is no reliable electricity.

Specifications
- Battery capacity: 2 + 5 = 7 kWh
- Usable AC (inverter) capacity: 6.7 kWh
- Maximum charging input: 1,900 W
- Maximum output: 2,400 W
- Approximate total cost: €1,100

By combining high-quality power stations with affordable universal LiFePO₄ batteries, we can deliver significantly more energy per euro—helping Ukrainian defenders stay powered when they need it most.

Over the past months, we have built high-capacity power stations based on the EcoFlow Delta 2 to help prepare Ukraine for the coming winter. With this new Pecron-based system, we now have an even better solution. It not only delivers excellent performance but also allows us to diversify our supply chain instead of relying on a single manufacturer.⁩
